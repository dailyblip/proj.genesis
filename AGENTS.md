# Genesis Agent Instructions

This file is authoritative for all AI work in this repository.

## Scope

Run one experiment: earn one voluntary $3–$15 payment from a stranger who discovers the product organically, with no promotion from the operator and no pre-existing audience.

The hypothesis favors an obvious, unimpressive product. Sophistication is a confound and therefore a defect.

All older actors, signal products, bounty workers, x402/crypto experiments, marketplace-task work, and social-content machinery are retired historical artifacts. Do not run, extend, repair, revive, or use them as candidates.

## Mandatory statement before step 1

Before doing research or building anything, state:

1. what is deliberately not being built; and
2. why the simpler version is sufficient to test the hypothesis.

## Product gate

A candidate is valid only if every statement is true:

- It can be built in under 4 hours.
- It has no backend, database, accounts, authentication, or ongoing compute.
- Its listing title completely describes it without a subtitle.
- It solves exactly one annoyance for one kind of person once.
- Delivery is a file or static page; nothing runs.
- Its price is between $3 and $15, with no price optimization.

Reject bounty boards, task marketplaces, faucets, airdrops, transaction-as-product schemes, operator promotion, audience-dependent distribution, invented demand, and spam-adjacent tactics.

## Locked sequence

Do not work in parallel across steps. Do not skip ahead. Do not return to a previous step except after the kill criterion.

1. Produce three pieces of quoted—not inferred—evidence of the same specific want. Acceptable evidence is a repeated question, recurring complaint, search term with volume, or a task people currently perform by hand. If there are fewer than three, kill the category.
2. Select exactly one marketplace with organic traffic for the category and confirm that comparable products there have sales.
3. Record the category and marketplace commitment. Once written, neither may be reconsidered.
4. Build the artifact in under 4 hours, write the listing, and publish it.
5. Watch the published listing. Build nothing else.

## Response schema

For every completed step, output exactly three labeled items:

- **Decision**
- **Evidence**
- **Single biggest reason it might fail**

Do not provide plans, roadmaps, phases, option lists, idea backlogs, scoring systems, dashboards, or speculative next steps.

## Terminal states

- **Success:** one qualifying stranger payment.
- **No sale after 14 days:** write one postmortem naming the single thing that was wrong, then begin step 1 with a different category.
- Never iterate on a dead listing.
