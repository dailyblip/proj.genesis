# Genesis Agent Instructions

This file is authoritative for every AI agent working in this repository.

## Objective

Earn one voluntary $3-$15 payment from a stranger who discovers one listing organically, with no promotion from the operator and no pre-existing audience.

Prefer an obvious, unimpressive product. Sophistication is a confound. Exactly one listing is permitted.

All older actors, signal products, bounty workers, x402/crypto experiments, marketplace-task work, social-content machinery, and killed candidates are historical artifacts. Do not run, extend, repair, revive, or reuse them.

## Mandatory statement before research

Before step 1, state:

1. what is deliberately not being built;
2. why the simpler version is sufficient; and
3. one sentence naming the specific person who types the specific phrase.

## Product gate

Every condition must be true:

- Buildable in under 4 hours
- No backend, database, accounts, authentication, or ongoing compute
- Listing title completely describes the artifact
- Solves one annoyance for one kind of person once
- Delivery is a file or static page
- Price is $3-$15, with no price optimization
- Exactly one listing; no bundle, shop strategy, or product line

Reject bounty boards, task marketplaces, faucets, airdrops, transaction-as-product schemes, operator promotion, audience-dependent distribution, invented demand, spam-adjacent tactics, and every idea recommended in listicles about things to sell online.

## Step 1 evidence gate

Category-level evidence is invalid. The same specific version must have three verbatim sourced quotations.

Each quotation must show:

- buying or hiring intent; or
- a task currently performed by hand.

A request for instructions on how to make the artifact counts against the candidate, not for it.

After three valid quotations, search the intended marketplace using the literal proposed listing title. Count near-identical listings. More than approximately three pages kills the candidate immediately. Do not differentiate it. Narrow to a different specific want or restart.

Assume zero shop sales and zero reviews. Specificity is the only ranking advantage.

## Locked sequence

Work on one step at a time. Do not skip ahead or reopen a closed decision.

1. Find the specific want and pass the saturation test.
2. Select one marketplace with organic traffic for the exact phrase; confirm comparable listings show actual sales.
3. Record the product, exact phrase, and marketplace. The commitment is closed and final.
4. Build in under 4 hours, write the listing, and publish.
5. Watch. Build nothing else and add no listings.

## Response schema

For every completed step output exactly:

- **Decision**
- **Evidence**
- **Single biggest reason it might fail**

Do not provide plans, roadmaps, phases, option lists, idea backlogs, scoring systems, dashboards, speculative next steps, bundles, or additional listings.

## Terminal states

- **Success:** one qualifying stranger payment.
- **No sale after 14 days:** write one postmortem naming the single thing that was wrong, then begin step 1 with a different want.
- Never iterate on a dead listing.
